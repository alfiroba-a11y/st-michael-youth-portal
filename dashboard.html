📿 The Holy Rosary: Complete Mysteries

All twenty mysteries, with a scripture reference, a short reflection, and the traditional fruit of each mystery.

🕊️
1. The Annunciation — Luke 1:26-38

The angel Gabriel greets Mary and asks her to become the mother of the Savior. Her humble "yes" changes the course of history.

Fruit: Humility

🤝
2. The Visitation — Luke 1:39-56

Mary hurries to help her cousin Elizabeth, carrying Christ to another household even before He is born.

Fruit: Love of Neighbor

🌟
3. The Nativity — Luke 2:1-7

Jesus is born in poverty in a stable in Bethlehem, showing that God enters the world in humility rather than power.

Fruit: Poverty of Spirit

🕯️
4. The Presentation in the Temple — Luke 2:22-38

Mary and Joseph bring the infant Jesus to the Temple to consecrate Him to God, fulfilling the Law of Moses.

Fruit: Purity and Obedience

📖
5. The Finding in the Temple — Luke 2:41-52

After three anxious days of searching, Mary and Joseph find the twelve-year-old Jesus teaching among the elders in the Temple.

Fruit: Piety / Joy in Finding Jesus

🌿
1. The Agony in the Garden — Matthew 26:36-46

In Gethsemane, Jesus prays in anguish, accepting the Father's will even as sorrow presses on Him.

Fruit: Sorrow for Sin / Conformity to God's Will

⛓️
2. The Scourging at the Pillar — Matthew 27:26; John 19:1

Jesus is bound and cruelly whipped by Roman soldiers before His crucifixion.

Fruit: Purity / Mortification of the Senses

👑
3. The Crowning with Thorns — Matthew 27:27-31

Soldiers mock Jesus as king, pressing a crown of thorns into His head and striking Him.

Fruit: Courage / Moral Strength

✝️
4. The Carrying of the Cross — Luke 23:26-32

Weakened and bleeding, Jesus carries His own cross to Calvary, helped along the way by Simon of Cyrene.

Fruit: Patience

🩸
5. The Crucifixion and Death — Luke 23:33-46

Jesus is nailed to the cross and dies for the salvation of humanity, forgiving even those who crucify Him.

Fruit: Salvation / Perseverance

🌅
1. The Resurrection — Matthew 28:1-10

On the third day, Jesus rises from the dead, conquering sin and death forever.

Fruit: Faith

☁️
2. The Ascension — Luke 24:50-53; Acts 1:6-11

Forty days after rising, Jesus ascends into heaven before His disciples, promising to send the Holy Spirit.

Fruit: Hope and Desire for Heaven

🔥
3. The Descent of the Holy Spirit — Acts 2:1-4

On Pentecost, the Holy Spirit descends on Mary and the apostles, filling them with courage to preach the Gospel.

Fruit: Wisdom and Love of God

👑
4. The Assumption of Mary — traditional teaching, cf. Revelation 12

At the end of her earthly life, Mary is taken up body and soul into heavenly glory.

Fruit: Grace of a Happy Death; Devotion to Mary

✨
5. The Coronation of Mary — traditional teaching, cf. Revelation 12:1

Mary is crowned Queen of Heaven and Earth by her Son.

Fruit: Trust in Mary's Intercession; Perseverance

💧
1. The Baptism in the Jordan — Matthew 3:13-17

Jesus is baptized by John, and the Father's voice proclaims Him His beloved Son as the Spirit descends like a dove.

Fruit: Openness to the Holy Spirit

🍷
2. The Wedding at Cana — John 2:1-11

At His mother's request, Jesus performs His first public miracle, turning water into wine.

Fruit: To Jesus Through Mary; Trust in His Providence

📯
3. The Proclamation of the Kingdom — Mark 1:14-15

Jesus begins His public ministry, calling all people to repentance and faith in the Gospel.

Fruit: Repentance and Trust in God

⛰️
4. The Transfiguration — Matthew 17:1-8

On Mount Tabor, Jesus is transfigured in glory before Peter, James, and John, revealing His divine nature.

Fruit: Desire for Holiness

🍞
5. The Institution of the Eucharist — Matthew 26:26-28; Luke 22:19-20

At the Last Supper, Jesus gives His Body and Blood under the appearance of bread and wine, instituting the Eucharist.

Fruit: Adoration and Love of the Eucharist
