🛡️ Our Patron Saint: St. Aloysius Gonzaga

"I am a piece of wax, to be molded by God." – Patron Saint of Christian Youth.

Feast Day: June 21. St. Aloysius Gonzaga inspires our youth community with his deep purity, dedication to prayer, and selfless service to the sick, showing us how to live a life totally devoted to Christ.
